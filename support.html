Common questions

Can Kindtime turn on Do Not Disturb?
iPhone doesn't let apps do that by themselves. In Kindtime, go to Settings › Silence your phone while you focus to add a one-tap shortcut that starts a block and turns on Do Not Disturb until it ends.

Why does iPhone ask for "full access" to my calendar?
It's the only option Apple offers for reading events. Kindtime only reads today's events, on your iPhone, to find free windows.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Kindtime Pro. Or in Kindtime: Settings › Manage subscription.

I bought Pro on another device.
In Kindtime, go to Settings › Restore purchases.

The timer kept going when I closed the app.
That's on purpose: Kindtime's timer is based on the clock, so it stays right even when the app is closed or your phone is locked.
