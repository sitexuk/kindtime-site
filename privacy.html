Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Kindtime is made by an independent developer in the UK. The short version: Kindtime does not collect any data about you. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server.

What stays on your iPhone

Your focus blocks, intentions, outcomes and settings are stored only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone backups, which Apple handles). They are never sent to us or anyone else.

Your calendar

Connecting your calendar is optional. If you do, Kindtime reads today's events on your iPhone to find free windows between them and, if you choose, to nudge you two minutes before a meeting. It only reads; it never creates, changes or deletes events. Event details never leave your iPhone. You can turn calendar access off at any time in iPhone Settings › Kindtime.

iOS describes this permission as "full access" because Apple doesn't offer a read-only option. Kindtime only ever reads.

Notifications

Kindtime schedules local notifications on your iPhone: a chime when a block or break ends, an optional daily nudge, an optional meeting wrap-up warning and a reminder two days before a free trial ends. Nothing is sent through a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. Kindtime checks what you've bought using Apple's signed receipts on your iPhone.

Shortcuts and widgets

Widgets and Shortcuts read the same information the app shows, on your iPhone, through a private storage area that only Kindtime can use.

Children

Kindtime is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support.kindtime@icloud.com
